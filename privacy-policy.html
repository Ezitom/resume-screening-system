Privacy Policy

Last updated:

EBEN is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

Information We Collect

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and resume documents submitted through the platform. Recruiter login credentials. Usage data such as page visits and interaction logs.

How We Use Your Information

To evaluate candidate applications using our artificial intelligence system. To enable recruiters to manage and communicate with candidates. To improve the platform's performance and accuracy.

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely. Resume files are stored in protected server directories and are not publicly accessible. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

Candidate Rights

Candidates may request deletion of their submitted data by contacting the recruiter who received their application. EBEN does not display candidate scores or evaluations to candidates directly.

Recruiter Responsibilities

Recruiters are responsible for handling candidate data in compliance with applicable employment and data protection laws in their jurisdiction.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at privacy@eben-platform.com
